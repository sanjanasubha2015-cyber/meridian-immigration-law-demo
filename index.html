Elena Marlowe

Managing Attorney

Elena advises individuals, families, and closely held companies on U.S. immigration matters that affect where a life, a career, or a business can unfold. The work is deliberate: she prefers a smaller docket, so that each filing is read as carefully as it is written.

Before founding Meridian, she practiced with an international mobility group and later with a Houston boutique focused on employment and family-based residence. She is admitted in Texas. Her counsel is known for its restraint — fewer promises, more structure, and a strategy the client can actually follow.

“The file should be able to explain itself. If it cannot, the strategy is not finished.”

Matters

Representative work.

01 Corporate · 2025

Executive immigration strategy for a multinational technology group

Coordinated visa architecture for a senior leadership cohort spanning four jurisdictions, sequencing L-1 and immigrant filings against a confidential acquisition calendar. The work included board-level briefing language and a mobility policy the company could reuse.

02 Family · 2025

Complex family adjustment-of-status representation

Guided a blended family through adjustment after an earlier filing had stalled on incomplete evidence. Rebuilt the record, addressed prior inconsistencies, and prepared the household for interview with a single, coherent narrative of eligibility.

03 Workforce · 2024

International workforce expansion strategy

Designed a staged hiring and visa plan for a privately held energy services company opening a Houston hub. Mapped role-by-role classifications, PERM timing, and consular versus adjustment paths so operations could grow without improvised filings.

Process

How the work proceeds.

  1. 01

    Consultation

    A structured conversation to understand facts, timing, and what success must look like — before any filing is contemplated.

  2. 02

    Strategy

    A written path: classification, evidence, sequence, and risk. The client leaves with a plan, not a brochure.

  3. 03

    Preparation

    Records are assembled with editorial care. Forms, letters, and exhibits are drafted so the file can stand on its own.

  4. 04

    Guidance

    From filing through decision, counsel remains available, precise, and measured — so the client is never left translating silence.
